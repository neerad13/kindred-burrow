The only adoption and fostering place for animals

Are you looking to adopt or foster these cute little animals? Then you are at the right place.

The Kindred Burrow helps you find current animals available for adoption and fostering. Choose as many as you like and go ahead with the process.

You can put up animals for adoption or foster care too

Why Choose The Kindred Burrow?

  • Comprehensive Vet Checks: All pets are health-certified and vaccinated.
  • Foster-to-Adopt Program: Test the waters before making a lifetime commitment.
  • Lifetime Support: Access to post-adoption resources and counseling.
  • Variety of Animals: From tiny reptiles to large dogs, find your perfect match.

The 4-Step Adoption Process

  1. Submit your official adoption/Fostering application form.
  2. Complete a quick virtual home review.
  3. Schedule a "Meet & Greet" session with your chosen pet.
  4. Sign the adoption/fostering contract and welcome your new companion home!

Quick FAQ

How long does the approval process take?
The average approval time is 3-5 business days.
Are the pets vaccinated?
Yes, all pets are current on vaccinations and are spayed/neutered.
What if I can't keep the pet?
Our adoption contract requires that you return the pet to The Kindred Burrow.
